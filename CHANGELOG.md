# Changelog

To publish a release, add a `## x.y.z` section at the top with notes and push to main.

## 0.3.0
- **Legacy**: a new page for planning what happens to your music if you can't look after it. Choose who would handle your catalog and a backup, note your will, trust and agreements and where each one is, list the accounts someone would need and where each sign-in is kept (never the password), write down where your masters, stems and session files are, and set out your wishes for the catalog, unreleased music, licensing and your name, image and voice.
- A readiness checklist shows what's left, and Manager reminds you to review the plan once a year (or as often as you choose).
- Print or export a letter for your executor or attorney with all of it, plus your releases with their codes and songwriter splits and where your royalties are collected.
- Legacy organizes things; it isn't legal advice. An estate attorney can turn it into a will or trust that counts.

## 0.2.1
- **Team** and **Contacts** are now separate pages in the sidebar. Team holds the people you work with, with what each one handles and a filter for check-ins that are due. Contacts holds everyone else, with the import options.
- Turning “Part of my team” on or off moves a person to the other page, and Manager takes you there.
- Links to a person from the Career Plan, Calendar, Coverage and Targets open the right page.

## 0.2.0
- First release published on GitHub. From now on Manager updates itself the same way as your other apps: it checks shortly after it opens and then daily, downloads new versions in the background, and installs them when you quit or click **Restart Now**. Check any time with **Manager › Check for Updates…**.
- **Targets**: publications, labels, radio shows, podcasts, playlists and festivals to pitch or keep an eye on, with how they take submissions, the people there, every pitch and piece of coverage, and a date to look again. Paste a website and Manager fills in the details. Includes a new label pitch.
- **Coverage**: save reviews, articles, interviews, premieres, radio plays, podcasts, video and mentions from a link, with a saved copy of the page, attachments, link checks, pull quotes for the press kit, and CSV import and export.
- Spotify editorial pitches, review requests, pitching several contacts at once, and importing contacts from the Contacts app, a vCard or a CSV.
- The app is now called **Manager**. Your data moves over from Artist Manager automatically.
- Install this version by hand once, because earlier builds can't update themselves to it. macOS asks again for access to Contacts and for notifications. Afterwards you can delete the old Artist Manager app; the ArtistManager folder in Downloads is no longer needed.

## 0.1.0
- First build, made on your Mac: onboarding and a career plan, Today, profile and brand, releases, press kit, email list, calendar, tasks, contacts, and approvals with an activity log and undo.
