# Changelog

To publish a release, add a `## x.y.z` section at the top with notes and push to main.

## 0.2.0
- First release on GitHub. Manager now updates itself the same way as your other apps: it checks shortly after it opens and then daily, downloads new versions in the background, and installs them when you quit or click **Restart Now**. Check any time with **Manager › Check for Updates…**.
- **Targets**: publications, labels, radio shows, podcasts, playlists and festivals to pitch or keep an eye on, with how they take submissions, the people there, every pitch and piece of coverage, and a date to look again. Paste a website and Manager fills in the details. Includes a new label pitch.
- **Coverage**: save reviews, articles, interviews, premieres, radio plays, podcasts, video and mentions from a link, with a saved copy of the page, attachments, link checks, pull quotes for the press kit, and CSV import and export.
- Spotify editorial pitches, review requests, pitching several contacts at once, and importing contacts from the Contacts app, a vCard or a CSV.
- The app is now called **Manager**. Your data moves over from Artist Manager automatically.
- Install this version by hand once, because earlier builds can't update themselves to it. macOS asks again for access to Contacts and for notifications. Afterwards you can delete the old Artist Manager app; the ArtistManager folder in Downloads is no longer needed.
