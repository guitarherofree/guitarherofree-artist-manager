# Roadmap

Version 0.1.0 covers the foundation the brief asked to start with: profile, career plan, release workflow, press kit, email list, calendar, contacts and approvals, plus the activity log, undo and in-app updates. The remaining capabilities already have a foothold through those pieces. The **Coming Next** screen in the app shows this same plan.

Phases are ordered by how much each one helps an independent artist day to day, and by what they build on.

## Phase 1 — Live shows and touring

**Works today:** shows, rehearsals and travel on the calendar with conflict checks; booking inquiries drafted and sent only after approval; venue, promoter and agent contacts with follow-ups; reminders to the people attached to a show.

**Next:**
- Offers: guarantee, door split, radius clause, hold/confirm status and deadline. Accepting goes through Approvals (`acceptOffer` is already a required-approval action).
- Tour view: route by date and city, drive times, gaps and back-to-back checks.
- Tour budget: travel, lodging, crew, per diems and backline against expected guarantees and merch.
- Advancing checklist per show: load-in, set times, hospitality, tech rider and stage plot (from the press kit), contacts.
- Merch inventory and a night-of settlement sheet.

## Phase 2 — Finances and rights

**Works today:** release budgets with planned, approved and paid costs (spending always needs approval); annual budget warnings; royalty organization memberships; songwriter splits and splits export.

**Next:**
- Income ledger by source (streaming, shows, merch, sync, royalties, grants) and monthly cash flow.
- Per-song registration status across PRO, mechanical and neighboring-rights organizations, with gaps flagged.
- Royalty statement CSV imports (distributor, PRO, The MLC, SoundExchange) and a collection-gap report.
- An accountant export (income and expenses by category and period).

## Phase 3 — Sync and partnerships

**Works today:** music supervisors, sync agents and brands as contacts; sync submissions and partnership pitches drafted, approved and tracked as outreach.

**Next:**
- Opportunity pipeline: brief, deadline, fee, media, territory, term and exclusivity.
- One-stop clearance checklist built from splits and master ownership.
- Instrumentals and stems tracked per track.
- A terms checklist before anything is accepted (through Approvals as a contractual commitment).

## Phase 4 — Marketing and media

**Works today:** release campaign timelines; the Spotify editorial pitch form with deadlines and checks; review requests and press, radio and curator pitches drafted to many contacts at once, with approval, sending via your mail app, follow-ups and reply tracking; a Coverage section for reviews, articles, interviews, radio, podcasts, video and mentions — filled in from a link, with saved page copies, attachments, link checks and CSV import/export — feeding pull quotes to the press kit; a Targets list of publications, labels, stations, podcasts, playlists and festivals to pitch or watch, with submission details, the people there, pitch and coverage history, and look-again dates; interview and statement prep frameworks.

**Next:**
- Content calendar with post drafts per platform.
- Ad budget pacing per campaign.
- Interview prep sheets built from the bio, the release and the outlet's past coverage.
- Finding new coverage automatically (needs a news or web-mention alert service you connect).

## Phase 5 — Daily support and creative feedback

**Works today:** Today view; conversation prep built from a contact's history; public statement framework.

**Next:**
- Tracklist and setlist feedback (flow, key and tempo changes, runtime), framed around the artist's stated preferences.
- Artwork checklist against distributor specs and the artist's visual identity.
- Optional demo and artwork feedback through an AI model service the artist chooses to connect.

## Platform

- Live connections (email providers, Calendar via EventKit), each visible on the Connections screen with its permissions.
- iCloud sync across the artist's Macs.
- Shared access for a team member with their own permissions.
- Developer ID signing and notarization in the release workflow by default.

## Needs an outside service

Ticketing and venue systems · distributor and royalty statement downloads · bank feeds · social publishing APIs · ad platform APIs · sync licensing platforms · an AI model service · an Apple Developer ID · an iCloud container.
